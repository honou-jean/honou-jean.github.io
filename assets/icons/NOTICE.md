# Icon sources

The portfolio uses local SVG copies so no third-party icon service is contacted at runtime.

- Python, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, R, Git, GitHub and LaTeX: [Simple Icons 16.27.1](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.27.1), CC0-1.0. Individual names and marks remain subject to their respective trademark rules.
- MATLAB, Matplotlib and LinkedIn: [Devicon 2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.17.0), covered by the bundled [MIT license](DEVICON-LICENSE.txt). Product names and logos remain property of their respective owners.
- Power BI: [Microsoft Power BI Icons](https://github.com/microsoft/PowerBI-Icons), provided under the repository's notices ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) for content and MIT for code). Microsoft trademark rights are not granted by those licenses.

SQL, FaceNet, Wav2Vec 2.0, EasyOCR, Tkinter and DistMesh intentionally use text treatments because no suitable canonical standalone logo is used here.
